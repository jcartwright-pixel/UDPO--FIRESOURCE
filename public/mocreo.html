MOCREO & Sensors

1. MOCREO key -

Paste the key here only, never in a chat or email. Once saved it is locked away and never shown again; only its last 4 characters show. A box left blank keeps what is saved.

2. Test connection

Reads the sensor list from MOCREO with the saved key. When it passes, the cooler readings start every 5 minutes; until then Cooler Temperatures is entered by hand.

3. Thermometers

Every sensor MOCREO sends is listed by the name you gave it on the MOCREO website. Pick your location for each one; it saves as you pick, and a location can be on one sensor only. Your locations are below: each box saves as you leave it, a location on no sensor is read by hand, Off leaves it off Cooler Temperatures and Remove (press twice) takes it off the list.
