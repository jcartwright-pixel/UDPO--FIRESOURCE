Side: Distribution shifts are the loading shifts; Plant Reports, Shift Notes and Shift Reports go by them. Plant Production shifts are kept for the plant production side. How Plant Reports use the shifts: a load counts for the shift it started loading in. Where two shifts overlap (2:00 to 2:30 PM, 10:00 to 10:30 PM) it stays with the earlier shift. A load with no Start time counts by its End time, and a load never loaded counts for the shift on duty at its depart time. Breakdowns, temperatures and pickups go by when they were logged or by their load. A time between shifts (5:30 to 6:00 AM) belongs to the shift that just ended.
