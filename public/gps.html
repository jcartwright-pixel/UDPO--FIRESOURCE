GPS Setup

Verizon Connect Fleet. Once the login passes Test, the app reads where every truck is every 5 minutes and the day's miles every 15 minutes.

1. Verizon Connect Fleet login -

Type these here only, never in a chat or email. They are locked away on the server and never shown again. Leave a box blank to keep what is saved.

2. Test connection

Test logs in to Fleet, saves the app's two read-only reports in your Fleet account the first time, and asks where your trucks are.

3. What the app reads from Fleet

  1. Where each truck is now: last report time, place, speed, ignition on or off, and the driver Fleet shows.
  2. Each truck's odometer and engine hours, and the miles it drove today with its first and last movement.
  3. The login is a Fleet sub-user that may use the API (an administrator role in Fleet). A sub-user made only for the app keeps working when someone changes their own password.
