4. Ask Verizon for everything

Type one Fleet unit, like a trailer you know has a reefer temperature, and press Ask Verizon. The app lists every field Fleet offers (search it below), then asks for that unit's record and every reading Fleet sends for it, and shows exactly what Fleet answers. If it runs out of time, press Ask Verizon again to carry on. It only reads; nothing in Fleet changes.

5. What the app reads from Fleet

  1. Where each truck is now: last report time, place, speed, ignition on or off, and the driver Fleet shows.
  2. Each truck's odometer and engine hours, and the miles it drove today with its first and last movement.
  3. The login is a Fleet sub-user that may use the API (an administrator role in Fleet). A sub-user made only for the app keeps working when someone changes their own password.
