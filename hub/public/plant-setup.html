Plant Setup

Plant & Distribution's set-up screens for a plant. Pick the plant, then open a screen: it opens in Plant & Distribution on that plant, in a new tab, and saves there as it always has.

Practice copy: the screens open the practice copy of Plant & Distribution.

Where each plant is

The MapMaker shows each plant as a pin and works out Miles from a plant from here. Type the street address and press Save: the map point is found for you. Or type the map point yourself.
