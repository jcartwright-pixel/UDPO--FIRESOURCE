## Home
id: home
screens: index.html

The **United Dairy Hub** is the one place to sign in and open every United Dairy app. Sign in with the same United Dairy Google account as Operations. You see a tile for each app you are allowed to use.

1. Press `Sign in with Google` and pick your United Dairy account.
2. Press an app's tile. It opens in a new tab, so the hub stays open for the next one.
3. To get the hub as its own app on your computer, press `Install the app` in Chrome or Edge.

### Which tiles you see
- **Operations** and the **Customer List**: everyone who is active on the Users list.
- **Fuel Reporting** and **UDMS**: administrators. UDMS keeps its own access list too, so it checks again when it opens.

> Administrators also see **Administration** on Home: **People & Access** is where people are added and changed.

!> Do not share the hub's link with anyone outside United Dairy: only United Dairy Google accounts can sign in.

## People & Access
id: people
screens: people.html

**People & Access** is the one list of who can sign in to the United Dairy apps: their role, their plants, and which apps they can open. It is the same Users list as Operations' **People & Roles**, saved the same way, so a change here is a change in every app. Administrators only.

1. Find a person with **Find** (part of a name or email), **Role**, or **Enabled / Disabled**.
2. Press a person's line. Their details open on the right.
3. Change their **Role**, their **Plants** (tick **All plants**, or each plant), or turn their account **Enabled** or **Disabled**.
4. **Apps they can open** changes as you go, so you can see what they will get before you save.
5. Press `Save`.
6. To add someone, press `+ Add Person`, type their name and United Dairy email, pick a role and plants, then press `Add Person`.

### Buttons
- `+ Add Person`: a new person on the Users list.
- `Save`: saves the person for every app.
- `Cancel`: closes the person without saving.

> Single-right changes (Allow or Deny one right) and the screens ticked for each plant are still set in Operations, Administration, People & Roles. Saving here keeps them as they are.

> Who sees which app: everyone Enabled gets Operations and the Customer List; Administrators also get Fuel Reporting and UDMS.

!> Do not disable someone to take away one app. Change their role instead; Disabled shuts them out of every app.

!> The primary administrator always stays an Enabled Administrator; the hub will not let that change.
